The Creed

Move — touch & drag anywhere on the left half of the screen (virtual joystick). Keyboard: WASD / arrows.

† Strike — attack. Strike an unaware enemy from behind for a silent assassination. (Space / J)

▼ Sneak — crouch: slower, harder to spot. Crouch in cardboard & trash piles to vanish. (C)

✋ Use — recover stolen antiquities, free hostages, burn smuggling caches, loot cash stashes. (E)

Guards have vision cones. White = calm, amber = suspicious, red = hunting you. Night and khamaseen dust storms shorten their sight. When your objective is done, escape through the brotherhood's glowing blue ankh — your extraction point.

Finish undetected for the Silent Blade bonus.
